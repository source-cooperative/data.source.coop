## Writing ADRs (`adrs/`)

Write ADRs in ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English). Keep them short.

- Use short sentences. Use about 20 words for a step and 25 for a description.
- Give one idea to each sentence. Use active voice and simple present tense.
- Give each term one meaning. Define it once, on first use, and do not use a second word for it.
- Do not use idioms, metaphors, or filler. Do not stack nouns. Do not put long asides in parentheses.
- State each decision once. Do not repeat it in Consequences, Alternatives, or Open Questions.
- Cut Benefits that restate the Decision. Cut Alternatives that no reader would suggest.
- Compare length with the sibling ADRs. ADR-008 (about 2,200 words) is the longest. Explain any ADR that is longer.
- Mark a number as an estimate when you did not measure it. Do not state a guess as a fact.
